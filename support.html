Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe anywhere on the screen to steer the snake. Eat food to grow and score points. The game ends if you hit a wall or your own tail, except in modes that say otherwise.

What are the game modes?

Classic: level up to face walls, moving blocks and a roaming bug. Time Attack: score as much as you can in 60 seconds; gold food adds time. Zen: no walls and no game over; biting your tail just trims it. Maze: hand-built mazes; eat 8 food to open the exit. Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, new every day. Shrinking Arena: the walls close in every 10 seconds.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button during a game. Bite also pauses automatically if you leave the app or get a call.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the main menu and switch off Sound effects or Vibration. Bite's sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch, and they won't stop music you're playing in another app.

How do I change the theme or snake skin?

Open Settings from the main menu and pick a theme and skin. Some themes and skins unlock as you play; locked ones show what you need to do to unlock them. Your achievements are under Trophies on the main menu.

Will I lose my scores if I change phones?

Scores, stats and achievements are saved on your iPhone. They move to a new iPhone if you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does Bite collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and the app doesn't connect to the internet. See the privacy policy.
