Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains how the Bite app for iPhone ("Bite", "the app") handles your information. Bite is developed by Dickson Ndung'u ("I", "me").

In short: Bite does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

Bite does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ins.

Bite contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software that collects data. The app does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, Bite saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your settings, such as theme, snake skin, difficulty, game mode, sound and vibration
  • Your best scores, statistics and unlocked achievements

This information never leaves your device through Bite and is not sent to me or anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under Apple's control and your backup settings. Deleting Bite from your iPhone deletes this information.

Information handled by Apple

Bite is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about your download and use of apps under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide me with anonymous, combined usage statistics and crash reports. These do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Bite is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you contact me

If you email me for support, I will receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. I use this only to reply to you and do not share it with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Bite ever changes how it handles information, I will update this page and change the effective date above. If a future version collects any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
